Emaar Charitable Association for Engineering Development

About the Association

Emaar Charitable Association for Engineering Development in the Madinah Region is a Saudi non-profit association licensed under No. (2023), specializing in developmental and engineering fields. It was established in 1446 AH to provide innovative solutions in building maintenance, restoration, and development.

The association aims to improve the infrastructure of public facilities and serve needy families by providing dignified housing and restoring buildings, with the participation of volunteer engineers and technicians.
